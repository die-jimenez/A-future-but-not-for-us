# A future but not for us
"A future but not for us" is a video game that explores the intrusion of artificial intelligences into activities we consider fun, from gaming to socializing. The game was developed and originally showcased as an unconventional arcade installation for an art and technology festival in Argentina, late 2024.

This is a port for PC of the original arcade design, which was specifically created for its own custom arcade controls.

Story
It's a "Vampire Survivors-like" with a cyberpunk aesthetic that evokes the 80s. The objective is to survive waves of robots while getting upgrades, but your companion, a small AI-powered robot will increasingly try to control your character. Who will be the last one playing?
